# MC GROUP Corporate Website

Static website (HTML/CSS/JavaScript). Open `index.html` or upload all files and folders in this directory to your web hosting root.

Pages: Home, About, Wines & Beverages, Expertise, Markets, Contact.

## Images
All 13 image files used by the site are in `assets/img/`. The updated images have replaced the old small mockup crops. Do not create a second nested `img/assets/img/` folder.

## Important before publishing
- Confirm MC GROUP legal name, business claims, telephone, and business email.
- Contact form requires a configured backend or third-party form service to actually deliver messages.
- AI-generated imagery is illustrative and must not be presented as actual MC GROUP premises, product brands, or owned vineyards.
